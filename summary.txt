MNIST Recognition by Neural Networks
====================================

Dataset: MNIST
Training data: torchvision.datasets.MNIST(train=True)
Testing data: torchvision.datasets.MNIST(train=False)

Models:
1. Shallow Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP)
2. Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)

Testing Results:
MLP Test Accuracy: 97.24%
CNN Test Accuracy: 98.84%

Note: The models were trained only on the training set and tested on the test set.
